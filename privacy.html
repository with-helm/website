HELM Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

Welcome to HELM!

HELM is a career exploration and navigation platform designed to help students better understand themselves, explore professional opportunities, develop their skills, and take meaningful steps toward their futures.

HELM is operated by With Helm, Inc. ("HELM," "we," "us," or "our").

We recognize that career exploration can involve personal information, including students' interests, experiences, aspirations, and uncertainties. We take the privacy of that information seriously.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, when we may share it, and the choices available to you when you use our mobile application, website, and related services (collectively, the "Services").

By using HELM, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy. Where required by law, we will obtain your consent for specific uses of your information.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly, information generated through your use of HELM, and certain information collected automatically.

A1. Account and Profile Information

When you create an account or use HELM, we may collect information such as:

  • Your name and email address
  • Your date of birth, which we currently use to verify that you meet the minimum age requirement for using HELM
  • Your college or university
  • Your major, academic year, and expected graduation date
  • Your career interests, goals, and preferences
  • Other information you choose to include in your profile

You may also create or access your HELM account using a third-party sign-in service, such as Google, Apple, or LinkedIn. When you do so, we receive certain information from that provider, including an account identifier and email address, to authenticate your account and enable sign-in.

For Apple sign-in, we also store a refresh token to support account authentication.

A2. Information from Uploaded Materials and Linked Sources

You may choose to provide HELM with resumes, links to professional profiles, personal websites, GitHub repositories, or other materials to help us understand your experiences and provide personalized career guidance.

Depending on what you provide, we may collect and process:

  • Resumes: Text extracted from uploaded resumes, including education, employment history, skills, contact information, and other details included in the document. We retain the extracted text but do not retain the original resume file.
  • GitHub: Publicly available information retrieved through GitHub's API when you provide a GitHub link, such as information about your repositories, projects, and technical work.
  • Websites: Content retrieved from up to five pages of a website you choose to share with HELM, which may include information about your projects, work, experiences, and interests.
  • Other professional information: Information you voluntarily provide through links, profile details, or other supported inputs.

Information retrieved or extracted from these sources may be used to develop your HELM profile, identify relevant skills and experiences, and generate personalized insights and recommendations.

Because resumes and linked sources may contain personal information, such as telephone numbers, mailing addresses, or other identifying details, we encourage you to review the information you provide and avoid including sensitive information that is not necessary for career exploration.

B. Career Exploration and Assessment Information

HELM helps students reflect on their experiences, interests, working styles, and professional development.

To provide these services, we may collect:

  • Responses to assessments and reflection exercises
  • Information about your skills, strengths, and experiences
  • Academic, extracurricular, volunteer, and work experiences
  • Career goals, interests, and preferences
  • Resumes, professional profiles, and other materials you choose to provide
  • Notes, reflections, and responses submitted through the application

We use this information to create and update your HELM profile and provide personalized guidance.

C. Voice Interactions and Conversation Transcripts

HELM offers voice-based experiences that allow you to speak naturally with the application.

When you use these features, your audio is transmitted to OpenAI, our third-party AI technology provider, for processing, including speech recognition and transcription.

HELM does not store your audio recordings. We retain text transcripts of your interactions so that HELM can remember information you have shared, build your profile, and provide personalized career guidance over time.

HELM does not request that OpenAI store or persist audio recordings. However, OpenAI may temporarily retain audio or related information in accordance with its applicable processing, security, and data retention practices.

HELM has disabled model training for its OpenAI services. Audio and other information processed through HELM's OpenAI integration are not used to train OpenAI's models.

D. Information Generated by HELM

As you use the Services, HELM may generate information based on your inputs, including:

  • Personalized career profiles
  • Summaries of your experiences and reflections
  • Suggested skills and strengths
  • Insights into your interests and working preferences
  • Career exploration recommendations
  • Suggested activities, connections, or next steps
  • Other AI-generated guidance and content

These outputs may be stored as part of your HELM experience and updated as you provide additional information.

E. Automatically Collected Information

When you use HELM, we may automatically collect certain technical and usage information, including:

  • Device type and operating system
  • Application version
  • IP address
  • Features accessed and actions taken within the Services
  • Session activity and performance information
  • Error reports and diagnostic information

We may use cookies or similar technologies on our website or within our Services to support functionality, understand usage, and improve performance. At the present time, HELM uses only cookies that are strictly necessary to provide the Services.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

Provide and personalize HELM. We use your information to create your profile, remember your experiences and preferences, and provide relevant career guidance.

Support career exploration. We use your assessment responses, reflections, and other information to help you identify strengths, explore opportunities, and develop plans for your future.

Operate our AI-powered features. We process information you provide to generate conversational responses, summaries, insights, and recommendations.

Maintain and improve the Services. We may analyze usage patterns, diagnose technical issues, evaluate features, and improve the quality and effectiveness of HELM.

Communicate with you. We may send service-related messages, account notifications, product updates, and other communications permitted by law.

Protect HELM and its users. We may use information to prevent fraud, investigate misuse, maintain security, and enforce our terms.

Comply with legal obligations. We may process or disclose information where required by applicable law.

4. Artificial Intelligence and Your Information

Artificial intelligence is central to how HELM works.

HELM uses AI-powered technology to process information you share, engage in conversations, summarize your experiences, and generate personalized career insights and recommendations.

To provide these features, HELM uses third-party AI service providers, including:

  • Anthropic, which powers HELM's primary conversational AI experience and processes information such as conversation transcripts, assessment responses, and relevant profile details to generate personalized responses and guidance.
  • OpenAI, which processes audio for speech recognition and transcription to support HELM's voice-based features.

These providers process information on HELM's behalf to deliver their respective services.

HELM does not use student information to train its own AI models, and neither Anthropic nor OpenAI uses information processed through HELM's integrations to train their models.

Anthropic currently retains information processed through HELM's integration for up to 30 days, according to HELM's configured data retention settings. OpenAI may temporarily retain information in accordance with its applicable processing, security, and retention practices.

Separately, HELM retains conversation transcripts and other information described in this Privacy Policy to maintain student profiles and provide personalized guidance over time.

We may use information about how students interact with HELM to evaluate and improve our products and services, subject to applicable law and the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

AI-generated content is intended to support your exploration and decision-making. It may not always be accurate or complete, and it should not be treated as a definitive assessment of your abilities or future career prospects.

5. How We Share Information

We do not sell students' personal information.

We may share information in the following circumstances.

A. Service Providers

We work with third-party companies that help us operate HELM, including providers of:

  • Cloud hosting and data storage (Render)
  • Artificial intelligence and language processing (OpenAI and Anthropic)
  • Speech-to-text transcription (OpenAI)
  • Customer support and communications (Alphabet)
  • Security and technical infrastructure (SendGrid, Expo, GitHub)

These providers may process personal information on our behalf to perform services for HELM. We seek to limit their access to information reasonably necessary for the services they provide and to use appropriate contractual and security protections.

B. Colleges, Universities, and Educational Partners

HELM may partner with colleges, universities, and other educational organizations to make our Services available to students.

We do not provide educational partners with access to individual students' private conversations, transcripts, assessment responses, resumes, or personal career profiles.

We may provide educational partners with aggregated or de-identified information to help them understand student engagement, identify broader trends, and evaluate the effectiveness of HELM.

We take reasonable measures designed to prevent such information from identifying individual students, including considering whether small groups or combinations of information could reveal a student's identity.

If HELM introduces features that allow students to share their personal information directly with educational institutions, we will provide appropriate notice and controls before enabling such sharing.

C. Information You Choose to Publish: Your HELM Dossier

HELM allows you to create a personalized professional profile, known as your Dossier, that highlights information about your experiences, skills, interests, and professional development.

You may choose to publish or share your Dossier with others.

Publishing your Dossier

If you choose to publish your Dossier, the information you include may be made available through a webpage hosted at helmpage.com. Other people may be able to view and share the information you choose to publish.

Shareable links

HELM may allow you to generate links to share your Dossier with others. These links expire after 14 days. During that period, anyone who has access to a valid link may be able to view the information made available through it.

Search engine indexing

Search engine indexing of Dossiers is disabled by default. If you choose to enable indexing, your published Dossier may appear in search engine results.

Disabling indexing does not guarantee that a Dossier will never appear in search results, and removing a Dossier from HELM may not immediately remove previously indexed or cached information from third-party search engines.

Reporting a Dossier

HELM provides a mechanism for reporting Dossiers that may contain inappropriate, inaccurate, or otherwise concerning content.

When someone submits a Dossier report, HELM collects certain information associated with that request, including a hashed version of the requester's IP address. We use this information to review reports, help prevent misuse of the reporting feature, and protect the safety and integrity of the Services.

Dossier removal and takedowns

HELM may review reports and remove or restrict access to Dossiers that violate our policies, applicable law, or other requirements governing use of the Services.

We may also remove or restrict access to published Dossiers when reasonably necessary to protect users, respond to legal requests, or maintain the security and integrity of HELM.

Important privacy considerations

You are responsible for deciding what information to include in a Dossier you choose to publish or share. We encourage you to review your Dossier carefully before making it available to others, particularly if it contains personal contact information or other details you do not want publicly accessible.

Even after a Dossier is removed or a sharing link expires, information previously viewed, downloaded, copied, or shared by others may remain outside HELM's control.

D. Legal and Safety Reasons

We may disclose information if reasonably necessary to:

  • Comply with applicable laws, regulations, or legal processes
  • Respond to lawful requests from government authorities
  • Protect the rights, property, or safety of HELM, our users, or others
  • Investigate fraud, security incidents, or violations of our terms

E. Business Transactions

If HELM is involved in a merger, acquisition, financing, reorganization, sale of assets, or similar business transaction, information may be disclosed to relevant parties as permitted by law and subject to appropriate confidentiality protections.

6. Your Choices and Control Over Your Information

We want students to have meaningful control over the information they share with HELM.

A. Reviewing and Updating Your Profile

You may review and update certain information in your HELM profile through the application.

HELM may suggest skills, strengths, or other insights based on your responses, but these suggestions are intended to support your reflection rather than define you.

B. Account Deletion

You may request deletion of your HELM account through the in-app account deletion feature.

When you delete your account, we will delete or de-identify associated personal information in accordance with our retention practices, subject to limited exceptions where retention is required or permitted by law.

Certain information may remain temporarily in backups or be retained where necessary to comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, or protect the security of our Services.

C. Communications

You may opt out of promotional communications by following the unsubscribe instructions in those messages, where applicable.

We may continue to send necessary service-related communications.

D. Privacy Requests

You may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, subject to applicable law.

Additional rights may apply depending on where you live.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Services, maintain your account, fulfill the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

This may include retaining your profile information, assessment responses, and conversation transcripts while your account remains active.

When your account is deleted, we will delete or de-identify personal information according to our applicable retention procedures, except where continued retention is required or permitted by law.

We do not retain audio recordings of your voice interactions.

8. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect personal information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, and loss.

These safeguards may include access controls, secure data transmission, authentication mechanisms, and protections provided by our technology infrastructure.

However, no electronic system or method of data transmission is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

We encourage users to protect their account credentials and contact us if they suspect unauthorized access to their accounts.

9. Age Eligibility and Children's Privacy

HELM is designed primarily for college students and individuals exploring their educational and professional futures.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without any consent or authorization required by applicable law.

If we learn that we have collected personal information from a child in violation of applicable law, we will take appropriate steps to delete that information.

Parents or guardians who have questions about a child's information may contact us using the information below.

10. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your state or country of residence, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including the right to:

  • Request access to personal information we maintain about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your personal information
  • Obtain a copy of certain information
  • Opt out of certain uses or disclosures, where applicable
  • Appeal certain decisions regarding privacy requests, where required by law

We will respond to requests in accordance with applicable law.

We will not unlawfully discriminate against users for exercising their privacy rights.

11. Third-Party Services and Links

HELM may allow you to access or connect to third-party websites, platforms, or services.

For example, you may choose to visit professional networking websites, educational resources, or other external services.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices of third parties that operate independently of HELM. We encourage you to review their privacy policies before providing personal information.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our Services, technology, legal requirements, or information practices.

When we make changes, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy.

If we make material changes, we will provide additional notice where required by law, which may include notification through the application or by email.

Your continued use of HELM following an update does not replace any consent that applicable law requires us to obtain.
