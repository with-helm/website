What is HELM

Career navigation, built for college students.

HELM is a new career navigation platform designed to help college students figure out what they want to do, and actually start moving toward it. Instead of asking you to pick a career path or sending you straight to a list of job postings, HELM helps you better understand yourself, explore what's out there, learn from real people and experiences, and take concrete steps toward internships, jobs, and other opportunities.

Why now

Made for a world where careers are nonlinear.

We're building HELM for a world where careers are increasingly nonlinear, the job market is changing quickly, and simply submitting more applications online isn't enough.
